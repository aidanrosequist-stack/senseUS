Every red / orange / yellow / green hex in senseUS

Every distinct hex value across src/ whose hue falls in the red-orange-yellow-green range (computed by HSL hue, not eyeballed — excludes grays, blues, and near-white/near-black regardless of a faint tint). Grouped by color family, most-used first. "Role" is my read of what each one is actually doing in the app, based on everything traced in this conversation plus a fresh check of each file.

Headline finding: green is the most tangled family — 10 distinct hexes serving overlapping jobs, including brand green (#6DA627, the logo) and a separate "success" mint (#52B788) that are easy to visually confuse with vote-green but are deliberately different systems and shouldn't be merged into a vote-color cleanup. Also found one real orphan: Compare.jsx line 380 still hardcodes the original (pre-Sept-3) wash green directly, missed by the tier-2 update since it's used as a border there, not a background.
