Honest caveat, not just a style note: text-shadow isn't recognized by WCAG's own contrast math (1.4.3) — a checker tool will still score these as the flat text-vs-background ratio, which stays low no matter how dark the shadow gets. It reads fine to real eyes at "medium" or "darkest" above, but if a future accessibility audit runs an automated contrast checker against this page, it'll still flag these specific spots. Worth knowing now rather than being surprised by it later.
