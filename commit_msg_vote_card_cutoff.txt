Fix vote card content getting cut off on shorter phone screens

Aidan noticed content clipped at the bottom of vote cards on his
girlfriend's phone, with extra unused space below the card on his own.
Two real causes, both in the vote card's own layout:

1. Vote.jsx's card container added a flat `paddingBottom: 74px` on top
   of a height calc that already subtracts the header and bottom nav's
   real heights. That padding predates BottomNav switching to
   `position: sticky` -- when it was `fixed`, extra bottom padding was
   needed to hold content clear of it; sticky already reserves its own
   space in the document flow, so this had become 74px of pure dead
   space, and -- more importantly -- 74px *less* height for the card
   itself on every device. Removed.

2. VoteCard.jsx split its two zones (question text, and the vote
   buttons/actions below it) with a hard `height: 60%` / `height: 40%`,
   both with `overflow: hidden` and no scrolling anywhere. That's fine
   only when content happens to fit that exact ratio -- a longer
   question or a shorter screen had no give, and the overflow was
   silently clipped instead of resized. Switched to flex: the actions
   zone (vote buttons, "Make Up My Mind", "View the Conversation", the
   hide-question link) now gets `flex: '0 0 auto'` -- its full natural
   height, guaranteed, since that's the functionally important part
   (this is what was actually getting cut off). The question-text zone
   above it gets `flex: '1 1 auto'` + `minHeight: 0` -- it takes
   whatever's left over and shrinks first if space is tight, instead of
   the two zones fighting over a fixed percentage.

No behavior/gesture changes -- the swipe/hold-to-vote code is untouched,
this is purely the static layout sizing.
